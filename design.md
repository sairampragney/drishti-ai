\# DrishtiAI Design System



\## 1. Design Goal



DrishtiAI should look:



\- Modern

\- Medical

\- Professional

\- Trustworthy

\- Technological

\- Calm

\- Minimal

\- Accessible



The primary visual style is glassmorphism.



Avoid excessive futuristic effects.



The website should feel like a professional healthcare technology product.



\---



\# 2. Visual Direction



Primary style:



Glassmorphism + Medical Technology



Characteristics:



\- Translucent cards

\- Backdrop blur

\- Soft borders

\- Subtle gradients

\- Rounded corners

\- Clean typography

\- Soft shadows

\- Minimal animations



Avoid:



\- Excessive neon

\- Gaming aesthetics

\- Excessive 3D

\- Overloaded dashboards

\- Excessive charts

\- Distracting animations



\---



\# 3. Color System



Use a calm medical palette.



Primary:



Deep blue / medical blue



Secondary:



Teal / cyan



Background:



Dark navy or very light medical background depending on theme.



Status colors:



Success:

Green



Warning:

Amber



Danger:

Red



Neutral:

Gray



Colors must maintain WCAG-readable contrast.



\---



\# 4. Typography



Use a modern sans-serif font.



Suggested:



Inter



Typography hierarchy:



H1:

Large and bold.



H2:

Strong section headings.



H3:

Card headings.



Body:

Readable and moderate size.



Small:

Metadata and supporting text.



\---



\# 5. Glass Cards



Glass cards should use:



\- Semi-transparent background

\- Backdrop blur

\- Thin border

\- Rounded corners

\- Subtle shadow
